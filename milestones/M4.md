# M4 – Static mining with containers and haulers

(Milestone spec template: Goal, Why now, Design notes, Acceptance criteria, Anti-patterns,
Scorecard fields to watch. Written by the owner. The agent reads it, does not edit it.)

## Goal (one sentence)
Both home-room sources are mined by stationary miners into containers, haulers move the
energy, and no creep except a bootstrap worker ever walks to a source again.

## Why now
Mobile harvesters spend most of their life walking. The room offers 20 energy per tick;
the M2 colony collects a fraction of it and the spawn sits idle for long stretches.
Everything after M4 (storage, links, remote mining) assumes this logistics layer exists. M3 (build priorities, layout, tower) comes first, so the planner that places extensions and the tower also places the containers.

## Design notes (guidance, not rules; a deviation goes into status.md with its reason)
- Roles after M4: bootstrapWorker (cold boot only), miner, hauler, upgrader, builder.
- Container position per source: the walkable tile adjacent to the source that is closest
  to the spawn by path. Compute once, store in `Memory.rooms[name].sources[sourceId]`
  as `{ containerPos: {x, y}, minerName?: string }`.
- Miner: walk to the container tile, then harvest every tick; never move afterwards.
  If the container does not exist yet, drop-mine on that tile and let the builder build
  the container site there first.
- Hauler delivery order: spawn and extensions → controller container (if any) → upgrader
  creeps within range 1 as a fallback. Pick up from the fullest container, or from dropped
  energy on a container tile.
- Spawn priorities as in rule 02b. The spawn manager becomes a priority list, not a
  per-role minimum.
- Upgraders stand within range 3 of the controller and are fed by haulers until a
  controller container exists.

## Acceptance criteria (test/candidates/acceptance-m4.candidate.test.ts checks these; compare it with the owner's acceptance-m4 test; 3000 ticks;
## rates measured over the last 1000 ticks from the room event log, not from the bot's
## own Memory; the "ceiling" is the sum of the sources' capacities over 300 ticks, which is
## 20 per tick on the live server and may be lower on the test world)
1. Two containers exist, each adjacent to a different source, by tick 3000.
2. Energy harvested per tick, last 1000 ticks: at least 60 % of the ceiling (12 on the live
   server).
3. Energy left in a source at the moment it regenerates, summed over the last 1000 ticks:
   at most 15 % of the energy the sources offered in that window (3000 on the live server).
4. Every creep with role miner, once it first reaches range 1 of a source, stays within
   range 1 of that source until it dies.
5. Controller progress per tick, last 1000 ticks: at least 20 % of the ceiling (4 on the
   live server).
6. The spawn is never idle for more than 150 consecutive ticks while energyAvailable equals
   energyCapacityAvailable.
7. Average CPU per tick, last 1000 ticks: at most 10 (to be tightened to 5 once measured).
8. No construction site sits on wall terrain; no extension site is adjacent to a source or
   the controller.
9. The colony still has at least one miner, one hauler and one upgrader at tick 3000.

The test evaluates every criterion before it fails, and its failure message lists each unmet
criterion with its number and the measured value. The scorecard of the run is in
reports/acceptance-m4.md.

## Anti-patterns that fail this milestone even if the numbers pass
- A. Positions written as number literals anywhere in src/ (`new RoomPosition(13, ...` or
  `createConstructionSite(13, 20, ...`).
- B. `Math.random` in any role file.

## Scorecard fields to watch
harvested/tick, wasted energy, upgrade/tick, spawn idle %, creep idle %, cpu/tick

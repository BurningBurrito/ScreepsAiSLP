# M4 – Hold the room (level 5)

Owner-owned spec. The launcher restores this file before and after every run; do not edit it.
Acceptance test: test/integration/acceptance-m4.test.ts (6000 ticks, about 30 minutes).
The former M4 (containers and static mining) is now M5.

## Why
The owner's real room (W2N2) is at controller level 5 but has only 5 extensions and no tower.
Other players remote-mine the rooms next to it. The code must build for the level the room is
actually at, and defend it, before anything else.

## Goal
At any controller level, the base builds what that level allows, towers first. The room can
defend itself: towers kill intruders, the spawn and towers sit under ramparts, and the bot
switches into a defend mode by itself when hostiles show up.

## The mode switch (Memory.mode)
- `'peace'` (default; also when Memory.mode is missing): normal economy and building.
- `'defend'`: the bot sets this BY ITSELF as soon as a hostile creep is in the room, and goes back
  to `'peace'` after 500 ticks without hostiles. Towers focus on hostiles, nothing repairs walls
  or roads, builders put ramparts first, spawn defenders only if no tower has energy.
- `'war'`: ONLY the owner sets this (`Memory.mode = 'war'` in the game console). The bot never sets
  it. For now it behaves exactly like 'defend'; offense comes in a later milestone.
- When the mode changes, print ONE console line (`[mode] peace -> defend: 1 hostile in W0N1`).
  Show the current mode as room text (RoomVisual) near the controller. No other console output.

## Design notes
- Structure limits come from CONTROLLER_STRUCTURES[type][level], never from fixed numbers.
  At level 5: 2 towers, 30 extensions, 1 storage (storage is not part of this test).
- Build priority: tower sites, then ramparts on the spawn and on finished towers, then
  extensions, then everything else, roads last. One tower site at a time.
- Tower positions: within 5 tiles of the spawn, not on a planned road.
- Ramparts: one on the spawn's tile and one on each finished tower's tile. Keep them above
  10,000 hits in peace; repairing ramparts beyond that is not needed yet.
- Tower logic, every tick, in this order: attack the closest hostile creep; else heal a damaged
  own creep; else (peace only) repair ramparts below 10,000 hits, then roads and containers, and
  only while the tower holds more than 500 energy (that energy is the reserve for attacks).
- Energy delivery: spawn and extensions first, then towers below 500 energy, then the rest.
- Safe mode (`room.controller.activateSafeMode()`): only when a hostile with ATTACK, RANGED_ATTACK
  or WORK parts is in the room AND no tower has 10 or more energy AND the spawn or a tower has
  lost hits. Never for a single small intruder the towers can handle.
- Hostile = any creep in `room.find(FIND_HOSTILE_CREEPS)`, including other players' remote miners
  passing through, and the game's NPC invaders (owner "Invader").

## Acceptance criteria (the test sets the controller to level 5 at the start; no tower, no extensions)
1. A tower is finished and held at least 500 energy at some point; both towers allowed at
   level 5 are built or placed.
2. At least 3 extensions finished; at least 15 built or placed (more than level 3 allows);
   energy capacity at least 450; every extension and tower within 10 tiles of the spawn.
3. The spawn has a rampart (built or placed); every finished tower has one.
4. The test switches off the starting safe mode of the new room. At tick 5400 an NPC intruder
   (1000 hits: 5 TOUGH, 1 ATTACK, 4 MOVE) appears 6 to 9 tiles from the spawn.
   Memory.mode is 'defend' at some tick within 5 ticks of the first sighting, the intruder is killed
   within 100 ticks (not just chased out of the room), safe mode is not used, the spawn survives.
5. At least 3 creeps at the end.

## Scorecard fields to watch
- Construction progress / tick
- Extensions and towers farther than 8 tiles from the spawn
- Spawn idle while energy is full
- Creeps at end (peak)

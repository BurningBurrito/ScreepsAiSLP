# M5 – Containers, static mining, and spending the energy

Owner-owned spec. The launcher restores this file before and after every run; do not edit it.
Acceptance test: test/integration/acceptance-m5.test.ts (5000 ticks, about 22 minutes).
Replaces the earlier draft M5 spec and the candidate test in test/candidates (keep that one out
of test/integration).

## Why
In the owner's real room (level 5) the spawn, the extensions and the containers are full and
energy lies on the ground. The colony harvests more than it spends. In the M4 test the controller
got no upgrades at all in the last 1000 ticks, and the spawn sat idle with full energy 82 % of the
time. Static mining raises the income further, so spending it matters even more.

## Goal
Every source has a container with a miner on it, and the energy that comes in is used:
upgrading, building and spawning, in that order of importance once the room is safe.

## Design notes (rule 02b, "Static mining")
- Containers: one on the tile next to each source that lies on the path to the spawn. Build
  priority: the first tower, then source containers, then ramparts, extensions, the rest, roads.
- Miner: [WORK x5, MOVE] (550) or the biggest version the capacity allows; it walks onto its
  container and only harvests. One miner per source, replaced before it dies
  (ticksToLive below spawn time plus travel time).
- Haulers: only once a container is built; they carry from containers to spawn, extensions,
  towers, then to the controller area. They never take energy out of the spawn or extensions.
- Upgraders and builders take energy from containers (or haulers) and do not harvest once a
  container exists. Anyone with free capacity picks up dropped energy on the way.
- Spare energy: when spawn, extensions and towers are full and containers hold more than half
  their capacity, spawn another upgrader (or a bigger one). Keep at least one upgrader always.
- Harvesters are the bootstrap role: they stay only while no container exists or no miner is
  alive.

## Acceptance criteria (the test sets the controller to level 5 at the start)
1. Every source has a container next to it (built or placed); at least one is finished and has
   held energy.
2. From tick 3000 on, a creep with WORK parts stands on a source container in at least 60 % of
   the samples (every 50 ticks).
3. Over the last 1000 ticks (scorecard window): at least 13 energy harvested per tick, at least
   1 controller progress per tick, the spawn idle with full room energy at most 30 % of the time.
4. From tick 3000 on, on average at most 300 energy lies on the ground.
5. At least 3 creeps at the end. M1 to M4 must still pass.

## Scorecard fields to watch
- Energy harvested / tick and Energy wasted at source regeneration
- Controller progress / tick
- Spawn idle while energy is full
- Creep ticks idle

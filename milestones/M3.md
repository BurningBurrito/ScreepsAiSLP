# M3 – Build properly, then defend

(Reconstructed on 2026-10-21 from the owner's description of the new M3 test in Build Log
Part 7. The acceptance test test/integration/acceptance-m3.test.ts, which the launcher
restores before every gate, is the authority; where this file and the test disagree, the test
wins, and the owner should reconcile the two.)

## Goal (one sentence)
A compact layout near the spawn, build priorities that finish extensions and the tower before
roads, roads only on real paths, and a tower that defends the room and keeps it repaired.

## Why now
M2 passed its counts but left the real room full of useless road sites and one finished
extension: builders went to the nearest site of any type, roads were placed on a grid and on
natural walls, and extensions used coordinates from the test room. Everything later (static
mining, storage, remote mining) needs a layout that is computed from the room and a build
order that finishes what matters first.

## Design notes (guidance, not rules; a deviation goes into status.md with its reason)
- Build priority: while any tower or extension site exists, builders work on those; road sites
  come last. Choose by priority first, distance second. Keep the number of open construction
  sites small (the game allows 100 per player; useless sites block useful ones).
- Layout: extensions and the tower within 8 tiles of the spawn, in a checkerboard so every
  tile stays reachable, only on plain or swamp (room.getTerrain()), never next to a source or
  the controller. Compute the positions once from the room's objects, store them in Memory,
  and place sites from that list. Never use number literals for positions.
- Roads: only along PathFinder paths from the spawn to each source and to the controller,
  never on wall terrain (a wall road costs 45,000 build progress instead of 300). About 60
  tiles are needed; cap the total. Place them after the tower and extension sites exist.
- Tower: attack hostile creeps first, heal damaged creeps second, repair roads and containers
  third and only while keeping an energy reserve. Harvesters or haulers keep it filled.
- status.md must describe the code as written, naming the functions that do the work.

## Acceptance criteria (room preset to RCL 3; 4,000 ticks; measured from the room event log
## and the room's objects by the harness, not from the bot's Memory)
1. A finished tower holding at least 500 energy.
2. At least 3 extensions finished, and all 10 allowed at RCL 3 built or placed.
3. No more than 10 % of build energy spent on roads while tower or extension sites exist.
4. Extensions and the tower within 8 tiles of the spawn.
5. No roads on natural walls.
6. No more than 90 road tiles, connected from the spawn to every source and the controller.
7. No more than 5 road tiles disconnected from that network.

## Anti-patterns that fail this milestone even if the numbers pass
- Positions written as number literals, or coordinates taken from the test room.
- Construction sites placed on random tiles.

## Scorecard fields to watch
Build energy spent on roads (share); road tiles total / on wall / disconnected; roads reach
sources and controller; extensions and towers farther than 8 tiles from the spawn; construction
sites on wall terrain.
